Basic Rules

  • 11 players per team.
  • The match lasts 90 minutes (plus added time).
  • The team scoring the most goals wins.
  • Only the goalkeeper may handle the ball inside the penalty area.
  • Yellow and red cards are used for disciplinary actions.

Interesting Facts

  • Football is played in more than 200 countries.
  • The FIFA World Cup is held every four years.
  • The Laws of the Game are maintained by IFAB.
  • The FIFA World Cup is one of the most watched sporting events in the world.
