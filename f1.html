Basic Formula 1 Rules

  • Drivers compete over a set number of laps.
  • The first driver to cross the finish line wins.
  • Points are awarded to the top finishers.
  • Drivers and teams compete for separate championships.
  • Pit stops are used to change tyres and make repairs.

Interesting Facts

  • Formula 1 began in 1950.
  • Cars can exceed 350 km/h.
  • The FIA governs Formula 1.
  • Every season includes races in multiple countries.
  • Drivers experience forces of up to 5G during races.
