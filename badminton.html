Basic Rules

  • A match is played as the best of three games.
  • Each game is played to 21 points.
  • A player or pair must win by at least 2 points.
  • A rally begins with a serve.
  • If the shuttle lands outside the court, the opponent wins the point.

Interesting Facts

  • Badminton is governed by the Badminton World Federation (BWF).
  • The shuttlecock is one of the fastest-moving objects in sports.
  • It became an Olympic sport in 1992.
  • Badminton is played in singles and doubles formats.
