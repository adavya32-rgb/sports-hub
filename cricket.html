Basic Rules

  • Each team has 11 players.
  • The batting team tries to score the most runs.
  • The bowling team tries to dismiss batters.
  • A batter is out in several ways, including being bowled, caught, or run out.
  • The team with the higher score at the end of the match wins.

Interesting Facts

  • Cricket originated in England.
  • The ICC governs international cricket.
  • The ICC Cricket World Cup is held every four years.
  • Test cricket is the longest format of the game.
