Basic Rules

  • Each team has 5 players on the court.
  • A game is divided into 4 quarters.
  • A basket is worth 2 points inside the arc.
  • A shot from beyond the three-point line is worth 3 points.
  • Free throws are worth 1 point each.
  • The team with the most points wins.

Interesting Facts

  • Basketball was invented by Dr. James Naismith in 1891.
  • The NBA is the world's most popular basketball league.
  • The hoop is 10 feet (3.05 m) above the ground.
  • The FIBA Basketball World Cup is held every four years.
